Outloud is a 100% on-device text-to-speech reader. No account or login is required to review the app.

HOW TO TEST
1. Open the app and tap "Paste a link or text", paste any paragraph, tap Listen — audio starts within a couple of seconds (after a one-time on-device voice download on first launch).
2. Or share a PDF / Safari page to Outloud via the share sheet.
3. Background audio: start playback, lock the screen — audio continues and Lock Screen controls (play/pause/skip) work.

IN-APP PURCHASE
- Free tier plays the first 5 minutes / 3000 characters of any document, then shows the paywall.
- "Lifetime" ($39.99 non-consumable) removes the length cap. A "Monthly" subscription is shown only as a price anchor.
- Restore Purchases re-unlocks on a fresh install. Tested in StoreKit sandbox.

PRIVACY
No data is collected. The only network request is a one-time voice-model download from our CDN on first launch; no user content or identifiers are transmitted.

ENCRYPTION
Uses only standard HTTPS — exempt (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false).
