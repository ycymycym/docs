Outloud reads your documents aloud — entirely on your device.

Share a PDF or web page from anywhere on your iPhone and Outloud starts reading it in a natural, high-quality voice within seconds. No account. No upload. No internet required once set up. Pay once.

WHY OUTLOUD
• 100% on-device — your documents never leave your phone. Works in airplane mode.
• Pay once, no subscription — unlock the full app forever with a single purchase.
• Built for getting through real work — papers, reports, articles, long PDFs.

WHAT IT DOES
• Share to listen — send a PDF, link, or text to Outloud and it reads the article body aloud (no nav, no ads, no clutter).
• Scanned PDFs too — on-device OCR reads image-only PDFs.
• Background playback — keep listening with the screen locked; control from the Lock Screen and Control Center.
• Pick up where you left off — every document remembers your place.
• Natural voices and adjustable speed (0.75×–2×).

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Outloud collects no data and shows no ads. Everything — text extraction and speech — happens on your device. The only time it connects to the internet is a one-time voice download on first launch.

FREE TO TRY
