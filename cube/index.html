Privacy Policy

App: Cube Buddy - Learn & Solve
Effective Date: 2026-05-23
Last Updated: 2026-05-23

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Cube Buddy - Learn & Solve (the "App"), operated by FLY SKY PTY LTD, handles your information. We are committed to protecting children's and families' privacy. This App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information, and contains no advertising.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) for advertising or tracking
  • Browsing history, usage analytics, or behavioral data
  • User accounts — the App requires no sign-in of any kind and works fully offline

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of data only on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • How many free 3x3 solves you have used
  • Whether you have purchased the unlock

You can delete all of this data at any time by deleting the App.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time, non-subscription in-app purchase to unlock unlimited 3x3 cube solving. All purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of transaction data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. No Advertising

The App contains no advertisements of any kind and integrates no advertising or tracking SDKs.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses only Apple's built-in frameworks (StoreKit for purchases, SceneKit and SwiftUI for graphics and interface). It does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be safe for children. Because it collects no personal information, requires no sign-in, shows no ads, and works offline, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
