Privacy Policy

App: Outloud
Effective Date: 2026-06-23
Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Outloud (the "App"), operated by FLY SKY PTY LTD, handles your information. Outloud is a 100% on-device text-to-speech reader. The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information, has no user accounts, and contains no advertising. The documents you listen to never leave your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) for advertising or tracking
  • Browsing history, usage analytics, or behavioral data
  • The contents of any PDF, web page, or text you open — all reading and speech generation happens entirely on your device
  • User accounts — the App requires no sign-in of any kind

2. Your Documents Stay On Your Device

When you open or share a PDF, web page, or text, the App extracts the text and generates speech locally on your device using an on-device neural voice model. Your documents and the audio generated from them are never uploaded to us or any third party. The App works fully offline once set up (see Section 4).

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of data only on your device using Apple's standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Your recent documents and where you left off in each (resume position), so you can continue listening
  • Your selected voice and playback speed
  • How much of the free preview you have used
  • Whether you have purchased the unlock

You can delete all of this data at any time by deleting the App.

4. One-Time Voice Model Download

To keep the initial download small, the high-quality voice model is downloaded once from our content delivery server the first time you use the App. This network request downloads the voice model files only. It does not transmit any personal information, document content, or identifying data about you. After this one-time download, the App reads documents fully offline — including in airplane mode.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time, non-subscription purchase ("Pay once") to remove the free-preview length limit and unlock all voices, as well as an optional subscription. All purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of transaction data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. No Advertising and No Tracking

The App contains no advertisements of any kind, integrates no advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses only Apple's built-in frameworks (PDFKit, Vision, WebKit, AVFoundation, StoreKit, and SwiftUI) plus a permissively licensed on-device speech model. It does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. The only network connection the App makes is the one-time voice model download described in Section 4.

8. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, requires no sign-in, shows no ads, and processes everything on-device, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
